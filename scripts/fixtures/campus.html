Student survey: which study spots actually work?

Over 1,200 students answered this semester's study space survey. The results show a clear split between spaces students say are productive and spaces that look good on the campus tour but get skipped when deadlines hit. The library's quiet floor topped the list, while the student union atrium came in last.

"The quiet floor works because nobody expects to be interrupted," said one respondent. Others pointed to lighting: spaces with harsh fluorescent lights or bright screens scored lower, especially among students who reported headaches or light sensitivity. The survey team recommends adding dimmable lamps to the Campus Center and moving vending machines out of the atrium.
